STANDING RULES

OWNER DECISION (20 Oct): the current board is a bullet slide with long sentences and is rebuilt from scratch each time. Useless.
Wanted: a detective-board shot where each new fact is ADDED as a new card to the existing board, so viewers can follow how the facts
were discovered and clarified. Every time the board is shown it opens on the board as it stands so far, and the new card is
visibly PASTED onto it and becomes part of it. Small title per card, no long text. Viewers get enough time to read; the film must
stay fully followable by ear alone (the board is extra information for people who want to look). Every board shot CONTINUES the
last one and never creates the same board again.

B0  AUDIT (read-only). Find the current "what we know so far" implementation (code, config, tests, how beats are chosen, how long
    it is shown, how it is rendered). Report how it is built and why it recreates itself. Write docs/detective_board_checklist.md
    with one row per requirement below.
B1  BOARD STATE. A persistent BoardState per film and language in the database (migration): ordered cards with id, type
    (person, place, object, document, event, statement, contradiction), title, optional date, optional verified image reference,
    position, rotation, links, source beat id, the spoken words that triggered it and the time it was added. Each board shot loads the
    last state, appends only new cards and saves the new state. Existing cards never change position, rotation or content (test:
    positions byte-identical across shots). No board is ever rebuilt from nothing after the first one.
B2  CARD CONTENT, STRICT. Title at most config board.max_title_words (default 6) words, no sentences, no verbs required; optional
    date line; a type symbol. Person cards use a verified real photo only if one exists and passed identity verification, otherwise
    a name tag with a neutral silhouette: NEVER a generated face. A statement card shows the speaker and 3-6 words ("Lizzie: in
    the barn"). A contradiction is two linked statement cards with a not-equal mark between them; the board never settles it.
    Card titles are produced per language from that language's own final text (never translated from another language): one cheap
    model call per language returns JSON {beat_id, cards:[{type, title, date, links_to}]}; validate with code: word limit, every
    name/number/date in a title occurs in the narrated words of that beat, links point to existing cards. Reject and report a card
    that fails; no retry loop beyond the existing bounded repair. Each card carries its source beat id.
B3  LAYOUT, "THE ROAD", AND THE PASTE. The board grows as a path from left to right in every language (also fa and ar; text inside a
    card is right-to-left there), time-ordered, with thin connection lines between related cards and branches for side threads.
    Deterministic incremental layout: a new card is placed next to its linked card in free space, with a small deterministic
    rotation seeded by the card id (a pasted look, no randomness between renders). Default style dark and minimal (paper-like cards,
    thin lines); the cork look is a config option board.style.
    EVERY BOARD SHOT HAS THIS STRUCTURE:
      (1) It opens on the board exactly as it stood at the end of the previous shot: all earlier cards already in place, calm
          (slightly dimmed), nothing re-animated, nothing rebuilt. The camera starts framed on the previous board or its latest region.
          It stays on this state for at least config board.lead_in_seconds (default 1.0 s) when the narration allows it, so the viewer
          sees what was known so far.
      (2) At the narrated moment of the new fact the new card is PASTED: it enters from outside the frame with a slight turn, lands at
          its place, is pressed on with a pin (a short press and the card's shadow tightens), and the thin string to its linked card
          draws itself. Paste duration config board.paste_seconds (default 0.5-0.8 s). The camera pans and slightly zooms to the new
          card while it is pasted.
      (3) The new card is highlighted for the reading time (B4), then settles to the same calm state as the older cards.
      (4) If the shot has 2-4 new cards, they are pasted one after another, each at the narrated moment of its own fact, never
          simultaneously and never with an old card moving.
      (5) The final state is saved as the new BoardState (B1) and is the opening state of the next board shot.
B4  TIMING AND READING TIME. A card is pasted at the narrated moment of its fact (word-aligned). A board shot lasts at least:
    max(config minimum, words on the new cards divided by config reading_words_per_second plus config margin) after the last paste.
    If the narrated beat is shorter, the shot is held longer through the existing audio-plan pause mechanism, limited by config
    board.max_hold_extension_seconds, and NEVER over speech. The shot may stay under the next narrated beat only when that beat is
    about the same card or thread; otherwise it cuts away to other visuals.
B5  FREQUENCY. A board shot only when at least one new card exists, at natural beats (a discovery, a contradiction, the end of an
    act), with config board.min_gap_seconds (default 90) and board.max_shots_per_film, and 1-4 new cards per shot. At the end of each
    act an optional overview shot shows the whole board zoomed out for a few seconds (no new card, no paste); the final board closes
    the film's board thread. Never two board shots back to back.
B6  SPOILERS AND AUDIT. A card is created only from facts the narrator has already said by that moment (spoiler firewall) and the
    existing visual auditor must approve each shot for the exact spoken words. Test: a fact from a later beat never appears early.
B7  RENDERING. Use the existing renderer if it can do this; otherwise the same deterministic frame-stepping approach used for map
    shots (headless Chromium via Playwright, per-frame camera and per-frame paste animation, ffmpeg). Fonts and safe areas as in the
    film; fa/ar shaping and bidi correct. Same input gives identical frames. The animation timeline (lead-in, paste, highlight,
    settle) is a pure function of the card, the narrated word time and config.
B8  UI. The story page shows the board per act (state after each act, card list with its source beat and spoken words) and lets the
    owner exclude a card and redo only that board shot (existing per-part rebuild). Export the final board as a PNG for the owner.
