OWNER DECISIONS (20 Oct)

   "host not in every clip" for reels; film appearances stay at their own limits).
2. The reel's first frame is its cover: Fereidoun's picture with the label (the words for "True Crime" in the channel's language) next to it.
3. Each reel has its own music with a volume that follows the content: lower while Fereidoun or the narrator speaks, louder in visual-only moments.
4. Every reel ends with Fereidoun saying a FIXED sentence in the channel's language, then the channel name and logo are shown.
5. The parts that are the same for every reel are created ONCE, stored properly and reused.
6. Cover labels and outro sentences below are approved by the owner. Music source: Eleven Music, subject to S7. YouTube related-video: checklist
   item if no API exists.
