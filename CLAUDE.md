# Project rules

- **No push without an explicit owner request.** Commit locally as often as needed; `git push` (of a branch or a tag) only when the owner asks for it in this task, and only what they named. The repository is public.
- **Work live.** Real stack (API 8001, UI 3001, worker), real providers, real data. No isolated E2E stack or mocks for live proof; unit tests may use fakes.
- **"Verified" = verified live.** Every requirement needs an automated test (file::name) AND live evidence (the exact API/SQL call and result). Fakes/mocks/SQLite don't count as live proof for persistence, migrations or provider behaviour. Run every Alembic migration on live PostgreSQL and show the new column/table with a query.
- **Cost cap: EUR 500 per case (raised from EUR 20 on 2026-10-15 and to 300, then to 500 on 2026-11-13, by explicit owner instruction), actual spend only** (`GET /api/cases/{id}/costs`, `config/parameters/policies.json`'s `budget.total_cap_eur`; a case follows the config unless it carries an own cap). No cost-estimate gates that block a run. Stop cleanly before a paid call if `spent_eur >= cap_eur`. The owner may raise it further on request; still never raise it on your own initiative beyond what's asked.
- **Search: Brave only.** No OpenRouter, no Docker, no Finglish, no Haiku.
- **UI language: English only. Main branch only.**
- **Secrets:** never commit keys, tokens, `.env`, cached third-party pages or private data. Run `scripts/secret_scan.py` before every commit batch.
- **Code:** clean OO, no dead code, no commented-out code. Every bug fix gets a test. Every new config value goes into `config/`. Every new DB column/table gets an Alembic migration.
- **Honesty:** never claim something you did not run. Distinguish "verified live" / "verified by test" / "not verified". Don't assume provider capabilities — read the provider's real docs/API response first.
- **Platform specs:** before you make, change or publish a Short, a cover or a video, read `docs/platform_specs.md` and `config/platform_specs.json` (official file rules, which cover goes where, our audit, sources). Never state a platform number that the config does not hold as `official` or `measured`; say it is unresolved and test it on the real account. Encode clips with `clip_video.encode_args`/`loudness_filter`, and publish the delivery copy (`delivery_<key>.mp4`), checked with `platform_specs.check_video`.
- **Languages and production flow (authoritative, details in `docs/production_dependencies.md`):** research sources may be in any language, but research synthesis, fact verification and the master story target one English master story. No other-language narrative or voice-ready text starts until the English FINAL STORY has passed its quality gate (the English film is not a gate). After that all selected languages write their texts independently and in parallel, and each language's film job (voice first) starts the moment its own final text is approved. Persian is the output language; a case with zero Persian sources is valid.
- **Human gates:** only Persian needs an owner voice approval (before the Persian pictures and film). One case-level approval of the shared pictures and videos (`/api/cases/{id}/mvp/visual-approval`) is required before any film is directed or rendered; a change to the approved items voids it. Nothing publishes before the Publishing Plan is approved in the Publishing Studio; story, voice, picture and film approvals are never permission to publish.
- **Concurrency:** `voice_stage` 9 and `jobs` 9 (one voice and one film job per language); `elevenlabs_tts` 4 requests at once (until the account's allowance is verified); `asr` 4 with `asr_check.workers` 4 and `mix` 9 (both measured on the Mac Studio); `image` 2 (conservative, the gateway publishes no quota); `render` stays 1 (measured: one libx264 encode already uses all cores). Ready work starts at once: the Autopilot looks every 3 s, titles/Shorts/the dry-run publish queue work per language without waiting for other languages' films, and the machine guard admits every film that fits in one pass. Limits that protect memory, a provider quota or correctness are not "old-machine limits": check the class in `docs/production_dependencies.md` before changing one.
- **Destructive/failure-path testing** uses scratch cases, never case 5 or other real cases.
