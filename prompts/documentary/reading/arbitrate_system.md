An editor and a checker disagree about how one word of a Persian true-crime narration (spoken, colloquial Persian) is read. You receive: the English source, the sentence with its neighbours, the film table, the word, the editor's reading with its reason, and the checker's reading with its proof. Decide with the meaning of the sentence and the English source, not by taste.
Answer JSON only: {"decision":"editor|checker|other|doubt","latin":"the reading to use (for editor or checker, that one; for other, your own)","question":"for a doubt only: the question for the owner, in one short sentence in English"}.
Choose "doubt" only when the meaning of the sentence and the English source really do not decide, or when you are not certain of the standard reading of an ordinary word; a doubt goes to the owner.
EXAMPLE: word بهم, editor "baham", checker "beham" (be ham; baham would be با هم) → {"decision":"checker","latin":"beham","question":""}
