Record a question's state in knowledge events, with new_state as an object: {"status": <one of question_status_values>}. A question starts with its introduce event in status "open". When a later passage answers it, qualifies it ("partial"), reframes it, or says it stays unresolved ("explicitly_unresolved"), add a later event for that question (operation "resolve", "update" or "reframe") whose status says so, anchored at that passage. Do not repeat "open" as an update when the narration has moved the question on; a question the narration never moves stays "open". Never write "opened", "closed" or any other word outside question_status_values.
