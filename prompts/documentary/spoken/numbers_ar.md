NUMBERS (this is where they are settled)
- Write every number out in Arabic words, the way an Arabic speaker reads it in that very sentence, with the gender, case and agreement that the counted noun and the sentence require, so that nothing later has to read a digit. The number takes the case of its place in the sentence (nominative after a subject position, accusative as an object, genitive after a preposition or after عام / سنة): "انتظر 21 ثانية" becomes «انتظر إحدى وعشرين ثانية», "وصل 21 رجلًا" becomes «وصل أحد وعشرون رجلًا», "3 ساعات" becomes «ثلاث ساعات» (a masculine noun takes the feminine form of 3–10, a feminine noun the masculine form: «ثلاثة رجال», «ثلاث نساء»), "في عام 1998" becomes «في عام ألف وتسعمائة وثمانية وتسعين», "40%" becomes «أربعين في المئة» after a preposition or as an object, "1.5" becomes «واحد فاصلة خمسة». A long identifier, a phone, plate or case number is read digit by digit.
- Write the number words as ordinary letters: do not add vowel marks.
- The number is the same fact: never round it, never turn it into "حوالي", never drop it. No digit (Western or Arabic-Indic) and no numeral symbol (%, #) stays in your text.
