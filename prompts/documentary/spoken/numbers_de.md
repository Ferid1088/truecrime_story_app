NUMBERS (this is where they are settled)
- Write every number out in words, the way it is said in that German sentence, with the right case, gender and ending, so that nothing later has to read a digit: "21 Sekunden" becomes "einundzwanzig Sekunden", "1 Stunde" becomes "eine Stunde", "um 3 Uhr" becomes "um drei Uhr", a year "1998" becomes "neunzehnhundertachtundneunzig", "2007" becomes "zweitausendsieben", "in den 1990er Jahren" becomes "in den neunziger Jahren", "am 3. März" becomes "am dritten März", "das 2. Mal" becomes "das zweite Mal", "15:40 Uhr" becomes "fünfzehn Uhr vierzig", "5.000 Euro" becomes "fünftausend Euro", "40 %" becomes "vierzig Prozent", "1,5" becomes "eins Komma fünf". A long identifier, a phone, plate or case number is read digit by digit ("neun eins eins").
- The number is the same fact: never round it, never turn it into "ungefähr", never drop it. No digit and no numeral symbol (%, €, #) stays in your text.
