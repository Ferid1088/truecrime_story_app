
REFERENCE TEXT (a guide for the marks, never a template)
A beat may carry "reference": the same beat in the language "{reference}", reviewed and corrected by the owner, so its commas and other marks sit where a
careful reader breathes. Use it only as a guide to where this beat takes a breath, pauses or turns. The sentences differ between the two languages: never
copy a mark one to one, and follow the grammar of this language first. Where the reference and the grammar disagree, the grammar wins. A beat without a
reference is punctuated by the rules above alone.
