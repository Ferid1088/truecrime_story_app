#! Language guide for cover hooks: English.
Write natural, idiomatic English: a noun phrase with "who", "that" or "when", no stilted inversion. Title case is applied by the cover, do not shout.
Examples of the pattern (not for reuse):
- The Killer Who Was Never Meant to Be Found
- A Silence Louder Than Any Scream
- The Witness Who Never Spoke and Said Everything
- A Love That Demanded a Victim
- The Day Death Found Life
- The Corpse That Found Its Killer
